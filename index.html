Our story

From one meetup to a foundation

The Dutch cloud native community began taking shape with the first meetup in 2016 and has grown steadily into a vibrant and inclusive ecosystem. Through the dedication of many volunteers, organizers and supporters, the community has hosted numerous monthly meetups and organized three successful Kubernetes Community Day (KCD) events.

To support sustainable, vendor-neutral growth, Stichting Cloud Native Netherlands was officially founded on 24 December 2024. The foundation provides structure and continuity to the community's ongoing activities.

Today the community includes more than 4,000 members and continues to grow thanks to individuals and organizations who share a passion for open source, cloud native technologies and knowledge sharing.
