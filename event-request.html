For organizers

Request an event

Organizing a meetup, conference, workshop or hackathon and looking for support from the foundation? Send us a short request. If your event fits, the board will get back to you with the detailed support request form.

Send request opens your email app with a message to info@cloudnativenetherlands.nl that contains your answers. Your request is submitted once you send that email. Does your email app not open? Use Copy request text and paste it into an email.

Sending a request does not guarantee support. The board reviews every request against the foundation's mission, policy and capacity. All events supported by the foundation follow our Code of Conduct.
